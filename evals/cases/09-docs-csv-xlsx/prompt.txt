From sales.json produce three reports using Solvra's document tools:
1. csv_write: out/revenue_by_region.csv with columns region,units,revenue (revenue = units*price summed per
   region), sorted by revenue descending, revenue with two decimals.
2. spreadsheet_create: out/sales.xlsx containing the raw rows.
3. doc_create: out/summary.pdf, a one-page PDF summary stating total revenue and the top region.
